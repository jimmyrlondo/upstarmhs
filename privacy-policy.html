Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices when you contact or engage with Upstar Mental Health Services.

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide through our forms, phone calls, text messages, and email, including your name, contact information, and details you share about service needs.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, coordinate services, communicate about appointments or referrals, improve our operations, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

SMS/Text Messaging Disclosures

When you opt in to receive texts from Upstar Mental Health Services, you consent to receive service-related text messages (such as scheduling, reminders, or follow-up communication).

  • Message frequency varies based on your interactions and care needs.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers or partners that support our operations, or when required by law. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No system is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request updates to your contact details, ask questions about communications, or opt out of non-essential outreach where available.

Children's Privacy

Because our services support youth and families, information may be provided by a parent, guardian, or authorized representative. We handle information in accordance with applicable laws and program requirements.
